# MAEO — AI Video Compression Research

**Undergraduate final-year research project | Competition showcase**

MAEO explores AI-assisted video compression with an emphasis on balancing reconstruction quality, compact representations, and application-relevant information. This public repository presents the project scope and verifiable development progress. The research implementation, unpublished technical methods, datasets, model weights, and detailed experimental results are maintained privately.

## Problem

Conventional video compression optimizes bitrate and visual reconstruction quality, but these criteria do not always capture information relevant to downstream AI tasks. Exploring how to evaluate and balance these objectives motivates this project.

## Project goals

- Investigate learned representations and reconstruction quality in video compression.
- Evaluate bitrate, image quality, and appropriate task-oriented criteria.
- Study adaptive allocation strategies under independently decodable bitstream constraints.
- Document limitations and compare against suitable baselines.

## Current development status

Initial codec and semantic-selection experiments have been conducted. Their findings remain preliminary and should not be interpreted as proof of superiority over conventional codecs. Further natural-video validation, reproducibility testing, and later-stage research are ongoing or planned.
